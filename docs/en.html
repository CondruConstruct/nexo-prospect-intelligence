BEFORE YOU START

Clear expectations.

Are these companies already interested?

No. Selection indicates a potential fit supported by public information. Buying interest is not confirmed. We do not guarantee replies, meetings, or sales.

What does the research include?

For each company: fit rationale, public source links with access dates, relevant public business contacts where available, and details that remain unconfirmed. Information may change after verification.

Can I request different research?

Yes. For other volumes, markets, or criteria, email your requirements. You will receive a custom quote before work begins.

Do you contact the companies for me?

This offer covers research. It does not include sending messages to the identified companies or arranging meetings.
